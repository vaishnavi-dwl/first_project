The Crimson Philosophy

Born in Bengaluru’s Gentle Night Air

Bengaluru has forever been India’s coffee capital, but as midnight descends over the rain-scented canopies of Cubbon Park, the city yearned for a deeper, more romantic sanctuary.

At Crimson Bites, we celebrate that twilight hour. We pair freshly roasted beans sourced straight from sustainable estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg with intricate French patisserie and comforting small plates. It is a space designed for midnight readers, deep thinkers, and nocturnal lovers of fine food.

Coffee should be as complex as a Bengaluru dusk, and dessert as unforgettable as a crimson moon.
